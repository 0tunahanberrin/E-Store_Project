# 🎯 Spinwheel Discount Strategy

## 📌 Compliance with Privacy Policy
In compliance with **Article 15 of the Privacy Policy** the earned discount code is reminded to the user via a notification during the cart/checkout stage. This approach is designed to nudge hesitant customers toward completing their purchase by highlighting the discount opportunity, thereby increasing cart approval and conversion rates.  

---

## ⚙️ Recommendation
To effectively capture buyer interest, the **spinwheel should trigger after the buyer has remained active on the page for 15 seconds**.  

---

## 🛍️ Benefits for Sellers
- On the web storefront, buyers are not required to sign up to complete a purchase. However, to benefit from the spinwheel discount and buy products, they must provide contact details such as their email or phone number. This allows the seller to gain a new communication channel for direct marketing, including campaigns and product recommendations (in accordance with **Privacy Policy Art. 8 & 13**). Consequently, instead of bouncing from the page, the buyer becomes part of **E-Store’s customer base** through the information shared to receive the discount code.  

- Winning a discount through the spinwheel creates a sense of control and luck for the buyer; this encourages them to stay longer on the seller’s page, driven by the fear of missing out on the opportunity.  

- In compliance with **Article 15 of the Privacy Policy**, the earned discount is reminded to the buyer via a notification during the cart stage. This helps nudge hesitant buyers to complete their purchase using the discount opportunity, ultimately leading to an increase in cart approval rates.  

- For buyers visiting the store via the **/storename URL extension**, a spinwheel that appears after a predefined duration can be highly persuasive in encouraging them to initiate a purchase.  

- By accessing critical **KPI (Key Performance Indicator)** data such as the ratio of buyers who spin the wheel after viewing it and the number of new potential leads who provide their emails the seller can develop more effective, data-driven strategies accordingly.  

---

## 👤 Benefits for Buyers
- Purchasing a product at a slightly more discounted price gives the buyer a **sense of profit and financial satisfaction**.  

- A random and easily accessible reward creates a **sense of joy and luck** for the buyer. This motivates them to track the spinwheel's reset period and encourages them to spin again.  

- The system provides the buyer with a manual **'copy code' experience**. Even if the discount is automatically applied in the cart, having a tangible coupon code gives the buyer a **sense of security and assurance** against potential technical glitches or system failures.
