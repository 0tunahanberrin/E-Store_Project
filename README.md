# 🎡 E-Store: Gamification (Spinwheel) Case Study

## 🎯 Project Goal
To increase seller conversion rates and buyer engagement on a SaaS e-commerce platform by implementing an interactive **"Spinwheel"** (gamification) feature. This project focuses on transforming anonymous visitors into loyal customers through strategic psychological triggers and data-driven design.

---

## 🚀 Key Deliverables

### 📊 Business Strategy
* **For Sellers:** Defined high-value benefits including **Lead Generation** (collecting emails/phones) and leveraging **FOMO** (Fear Of Missing Out) to increase time-on-site.
* **For Buyers:** Focused on **Financial Satisfaction** and the "joy of winning" to drive instant purchase decisions.
* **Detailed Analysis:** [Strategic Value & Benefits Analysis](https://github.com/0tunahanberrin/E-Store_Project/blob/main/Strategic%20Value%20%26%20Benefits%20Analysis.md)

### 📝 Technical Documentation
* Authored prioritized **User Stories** and **Acceptance Criteria** in compliance with data privacy policies.
* **Project Documents:** [Technical Documentation & User Stories](https://github.com/0tunahanberrin/E-Store_Project/tree/main/Technical%20Documentation)

### 🎨 UI/UX Design
Developed high-fidelity mock-ups for a seamless end-to-end experience:
* **Seller Management Panel:** A comprehensive dashboard for campaign setup and real-time analytics.
* **Mobile Storefront:** A responsive, user-friendly game interface designed for high engagement.
* **Design Assets:** [UI/UX Design Mock-ups](https://github.com/0tunahanberrin/E-Store_Project/tree/main/UI-UX%20Design)

---

## 💡 Impact
A **data-driven solution** that utilizes strategic reward systems to drive purchase intent and enhance customer retention by converting casual visitors into loyal buyers.
